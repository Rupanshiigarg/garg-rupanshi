# Editorial Rose Recruiter Portfolio

## Goal
Transform the existing dark-crimson portfolio into a light-red, Awwwards-caliber experience that helps recruiters understand Rupanshi’s SDE fit quickly.

## Visual direction
- Apply the selected **Editorial rose aesthetic** throughout the site.
- Use a warm-white and pale-blush canvas, deep ink typography, crimson focal accents, fine red rules, and subtle editorial depth.
- Preserve the technical identity through restrained grids, system readouts, terminal details, and precise micro-motion rather than returning to a generic résumé layout.
- Keep contrast strong and typography highly legible across desktop and mobile.

## Recruiter-first structure
- Keep Rupanshi Garg and “Software Development Engineer” as the immediate first-screen signals.
- Preserve the existing resume-backed content, project evidence, coding achievements, experience, education, contact actions, and downloadable résumé.
- Strengthen scanability through clearer section hierarchy, compact proof points, concise project outcomes, and consistent technical metadata.
- Do not invent metrics, links, employers, experience, or achievements.

## Implementation
- Rework the global design tokens from dark crimson to the selected light editorial palette, including surfaces, text, borders, panels, controls, grids, selection, and shadows.
- Refine the first viewport to echo the chosen composition while retaining Rupanshi’s real name, SDE positioning, actions, and system-signal motif.
- Update project visuals, proof metrics, experience, education, navigation, and contact areas so they feel like one cohesive light-red art direction.
- Keep motion restrained and purposeful, with full reduced-motion support.
- Retain semantic controls, one clear page heading, accessible contrast, keyboard usability, and existing metadata.

## Validation
- Check the complete page at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Verify no horizontal overflow, clipped text, overlap, broken navigation, or unreadable states.
- Test the mobile menu, section links, email action, and résumé download.
- Confirm there are no browser console errors and that the final page remains fast to scan for recruiters.
